Flight Simulator Experiences in Sweden

A Royal Guide for Aviation Enthusiasts

About Flight Simulators

Flight simulators provide a safe way to experience the thrill of piloting an aircraft. Whether you want to practice procedures or simply enjoy flying, Sweden offers several high-quality simulator options.

Where to Fly in Sweden

  • Aeroseum (Gothenburg) – Located in a declassified military bunker, Aeroseum offers a unique setting to try helicopter and aircraft simulators.
  • Scandinavian Xperience (Stockholm) – Provides professional-grade commercial aircraft simulators for enthusiasts and pilots alike.
  • Malmo Aviation Simulator Center – Offers modern jet simulators, perfect for experiencing takeoffs and landings from Swedish airports.

Luxury Packages

For a truly royal treatment, several providers offer premium packages that include extended flight time, personalized instruction, and gourmet refreshments in an exclusive lounge.

Helpful Tips

  1. Book your session in advance, as popular simulators can fill up quickly.
  2. Bring comfortable clothing for extended sessions.
  3. Don't hesitate to ask instructors about specific procedures or maneuvers.

Contact

Many simulators can be booked online. If you have questions about specific experiences, contact the venues directly or reach out to your chosen provider for details on group events and corporate packages.
